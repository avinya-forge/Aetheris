# Aetheris: Temporal Intelligence & Environmental Sentinel

> **North Star**: To eliminate "Information Fatigue" by replacing sensationalist news feeds with a high-integrity **Temporal Intelligence Engine**. Aetheris traverses the "Pulse" of time—from historical macro-trends to real-time physics and logic-based probability cones—using primary sources to predict cascading impacts on safety and society.

---

## 1. The Pulse: Temporal Axis
Users do not scroll; they **traverse** a 3D-mapped timeline across four critical tiers:

| Tier | Focus | Timeframe | Engine Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Past** | Rapid Response | 2h – 48h | Wire deduplication + 30-word extractive factual briefs. |
| **History** | Trend Analysis | 1w – 1m | Macro-Cluster identification + geopolitical shift tracking. |
| **Present** | Real-Time Flash | Live | Kp Index (Aurora), Heatwave alerts, and breaking wires. |
| **Horizon** | Predictive Modeling | 0 – 48h | Logic-based Probability Cones (Ghost Cards); strictly non-speculative. |

---

## 2. Kinetic Atlas: Visual Integrity
The UI is a high-performance WebGL environment governed by strict aesthetic and functional rules:

### Tiered Zoom Logic
| Level | View | Content Detail |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **L1: Orbital** (zoom < 4) | Global / Space | Solar storms, trade routes, conflict zones, aurora extent. |
| **L2: National** (zoom 4–8) | Country | Legislative shifts, nationwide weather fronts, regional alerts. |
| **L3: Local** (zoom > 8) | County | Fire alerts, roadworks, heat domes, hyper-local warnings. |

### Visual Non-Negotiables
- **Zero Raster**: No stock photos, no list views.
- **Vector Glyphs Only**: SVG, stroke-only, 24×24 viewBox for all markers.
- **Chromodynamic Atmosphere**: UI background dynamically shifts with Kp Index (0–9).
  - *Extreme (Kp≥8)*: Deep Purple | *High (Kp≥6)*: Electric Violet | *Moderate (Kp≥5)*: Indigo.
  - *Amber Tint Overlay*: Active heatwave (temp ≥ 40°C) triggers atmospheric tint.

## 3. Data Ground Truth Protocol
Aetheris ignores "opinion" and focuses on "physics" and "primary logs."

### Primary Sources (Zero-Cost)
- **Environmental**: Open-Meteo (Weather/AQI), NOAA SWPC (Space Weather), NASA DONKI (Solar Events).
- **Geopolitical**: GDELT Project (Global articles in 100+ languages, wire-level proxy).
- **AI Synthesis**: Gemini 1.5 Flash (Factual extraction only).

---

## 4. AI Guardrails & Safety
To prevent hallucination, the system enforces logic-based enrichment:
- **Extractive Synthesis**: Summaries must be ≤ 30 words and derived from 20+ validated sources.
- **Safety Sentinel**: Autonomous injection of warnings if physics thresholds are met (e.g., wind ≥ 100 km/h).
- **Probability Cones**: Required `patternMatchId` and `isSpeculative: false` to render a Ghost Card. Capped at 95% likelihood.

---

## 5. Zero-Cost Reliability
Aetheris is architected for maximum resilience at -bash/mo cost:
- **Edge Serving**: Cloudflare Workers & Pages for sub-50ms global latency.
- **Offline-First**: Service Worker (sw.js) caching ensures PWA functionality during network degradation.
- **Autonomous Lifecycle**: 1-minute ingest cycles via Cloudflare Cron Triggers.
