# Aetheris Beta Guide

Welcome to the Aetheris Beta! This guide will help you understand how to navigate and interpret the temporal intelligence system.

## The Pulse Time-Axis
Our interface shifts away from traditional vertical scrolling to a 3D-mapped timeline:

- **Past (0-2h)**: High-confidence meteorological and space weather data.
- **History (2h-24h)**: Deduplicated news and systemic impacts mapped geographically.
- **Present (Nowcast)**: Real-time kinetic conditions generated via interpolation.
- **Horizon (Future)**: Probability cones showing speculative predictive models based on current trends.

## Chromodynamic Atmosphere
The background color of the interface changes dynamically based on the current planetary Kp-Index (Geomagnetic Activity):

- **Kp < 5 (Normal)**: Dark slate gray. Normal conditions.
- **Kp >= 5 (Moderate)**: Indigo. Minor to moderate geomagnetic storming.
- **Kp >= 6 (High)**: Electric violet. Strong storm conditions. Watch for power grid anomalies or auroras.
- **Kp >= 8 (Extreme)**: Deep purple. Severe disruptions likely.

## Interacting with Ghost Cards
Future events and historical unverified scenarios are represented as "Ghost Cards". These elements fade out as their probability confidence score drops. They act as "what if" scenarios rather than concrete news.
